# MAPS WITH TEETH — CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **Precision Over Generalization**: We do not publish generic directory cards. We publish verified operational mechanics, exact eligibility thresholds, statutory citations, and "The Catch" (frictions and exceptions).
2. **Never Guarantee the Uncontrollable**: We clearly distinguish statutory entitlements from discretionary benevolence funds and local committee allocations.
3. **Zero Fabricated Evidence**: Every public operational claim must be anchored to an authoritative source or direct agency interview.
4. **Safety & Notification Realism**: We never promise "zero abuser notification" when carrier billing or account updates could alert an account holder.
5. **Client-Controlled Privacy**: No survivor intake data is stored unencrypted on central servers.
