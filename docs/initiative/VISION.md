# MAPS WITH TEETH — INITIATIVE VISION

Maps With Teeth is a barrier-first resource intelligence and continuity infrastructure initiative designed to resolve two structural failures in domestic crisis recovery:

1. **Finding A Way Through**: Uncovering non-obvious, legally defensible, and lateral resource pathways outside conventional, overwhelmed shelter waitlists (statutory rights, 501(c)(3) industry benevolence funds, deposit waivers, emergency relocation programs).
2. **Carrying Context Forward**: Preserving and carrying factual intake context across disconnected agencies so survivors do not have to retell trauma or start over at every institutional door.
