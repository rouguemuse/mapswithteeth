# MEMORANDUM: SYSTEMIC CONTINUITY RECOMMENDATIONS FOR DFPS SUNSET REVIEW
**To**: Members & Staff, Texas Sunset Advisory Commission  
**From**: Maps With Teeth Public-Interest Systems Research  
**Date**: October 2026 (For November 18, 2026 Public Hearing)  
**Subject**: Interagency Continuity, Closed-Loop Referral Tracking, and Context-Before-Closure Practices at the Department of Family and Protective Services (DFPS)  

---

## I. PROBLEM STATEMENT: THE ADMINISTRATIVE SEAM DEFICIT

In child-safety and family violence matters, DFPS investigations frequently intersect with local law enforcement offense reports, municipal criminal proceedings, civil protective orders, school district attendance flags, and victim service providers.

Under current DFPS operating practices:
1. **Referral Termination at Dispatch**: Outbound referrals to community providers or law enforcement are categorized as "referred out" and closed without verifying whether the receiving agency acknowledged receipt, possessed capacity, or initiated contact.
2. **Fragmented Screen-Outs**: Separate reports involving the same household or children across adjacent jurisdictions are evaluated in isolation, obscuring cumulative risk patterns.
3. **Closure Without Context Verification**: Cases are closed as "unable to determine" or "screened out" without an administrative audit confirming whether critical investigative files held by neighboring agencies or family courts were queried.

---

## II. STATUTORY PRECEDENTS UNDER EXISTING TEXAS LAW

Texas law already recognizes that child safety requires structured interagency coordination:
- **Tex. Fam. Code §§ 261.301(f) & 261.3011**: Mandates joint investigations and collaborative guidelines between DFPS and local law enforcement.
- **Tex. Fam. Code §§ 264.403 & 264.4031**: Establishes binding interagency MOUs and working protocols for Children's Advocacy Centers (CACs), covering intake, referral tracking, and timely information exchange.
- **Tex. Fam. Code § 264.406(e)**: Authorizes bounded exchange of confidential records among multidisciplinary team members.

**The Sunset Opportunity**: The Commission can extend these proven multidisciplinary coordination principles beyond CAC boundaries to address everyday interagency handoffs across standard DFPS casework.

---

## III. ACTIONABLE RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUNSET DIRECTIVES

Maps With Teeth recommends that the Sunset Advisory Commission include the following structural directives in its staff recommendations for DFPS:

### 1. Mandate Closed-Loop Referral Tracking (Standard Module 02)
- **Directive**: Require DFPS casework management systems to log and report referral acknowledgment metrics:
  - *Outbound Referral Dispatch Timestamp*
  - *Receiving Entity Acknowledgment Status (Accepted / Capacity Full / Declined)*
  - *Designated Decision Owner at Receiving Entity*
- **SLA**: Mandate a 48-hour electronic acknowledgment benchmark. Referrals remaining unacknowledged after 14 days must trigger an automated internal supervisor alert.

### 2. Implement "Context-Before-Closure" Administrative Checklist (Standard Module 06)
- **Directive**: Prior to finalizing administrative closure, screen-out, or declination of a qualifying child-safety report, the caseworker and supervisor must complete a standardized pre-closure audit recording:
  - *Which related law enforcement or court records were examined;*
  - *Which records were requested but unavailable;*
  - *Which related agencies were checked for concurrent filings;*
  - *The specific administrative rationale for closure.*
- **Integrity Boundary**: This checklist tracks process completeness and due diligence; it does not force substantive findings of fact.

### 3. Establish Cumulative Multi-Contact Review Thresholds (Standard Module 04)
- **Directive**: Require supervisory case review whenever three or more fragmented reports, screen-outs, or intake declinations occur regarding the same household across a 90-day window.
- **Safeguard**: Evaluations must be conducted directly by human supervisors and multidisciplinary liaisons—**not** by automated, opaque AI risk algorithms.

### 4. Support Low-Risk 90-Day Synthetic Mechanics Pilot (Standard Module 03/05)
- **Directive**: Direct DFPS to collaborate with 2–3 regional victim-service and civil legal-aid partners on a 90-day closed-loop referral pilot using synthetic data to stress-test receipt protocols and decision-ownership assignments.

---

## IV. CONCLUSION

These recommendations require **no new centralized databases, no invasive public registries, and no expansion of coercive agency authority.** They establish fundamental administrative accountability at institutional seams, ensuring that child safety and family stability do not vanish in the void between agencies.
