Core Institutional Thesis
Survivors do not drop out because their cases lack substance. They drop out because institutional friction, repeated retelling, lost paper trails, and unrecorded gatekeeper refusals inflict compounded trauma before formal adjudication ever begins. Maps With Teeth provides immutable, participant-held continuity infrastructure that turns fragmented institutional dead-ends into auditable, accountable records.
The System Loop Institutional Navigation Realities
Current Fragmented Reality With Maps With Teeth
Forced Repetition: Survivors retell trauma 5–11 times across unlinked legal and advocacy silos. Single Digital Bridge: Client-side cryptographic intake shared securely under participant-controlled consent.
Untracked Turnaways: Verbal referrals and desk rejections evaporate with zero record of contact. Continuity Contact Record: Standardized, timestamped contact artifact created regardless of agency cooperation.
Agency Liability Panic: Institutions refuse receipts fearing admission of liability or evidence certification. Strict Non-Implication: 5 statutory disclaimers legally decouple proof of contact from merits or liability.
Opaque Routing: Dead-end referrals remain hidden; next survivors sent down the exact same hostile routes. Resource Graph & Bad Maps: Institutional friction and refusal patterns surface to guide survivors away from dead ends.
The Core Architecture Three Foundational Pillars
1. The Bridge (Zero-Knowledge Intake) Client-Side Cryptography
Survivor-held, privacy-first intake protocol. Structured disclosures are encrypted in-browser, preventing unauthorized institutional indexing while maintaining granular survivor consent across referrals.
2. Continuity Contact Record Standardized Contact Spec
Generates a formal, immutable touchpoint artifact documenting date, actor, and handling status across 3 verification tiers: Participant-Recorded, Agency-Acknowledged, and Partner-Role-Verified.
3. Living Resource Graph & Bad Maps Accountability Intelligence
Aggregates verified routing pathways while systematically flagging "Bad Maps"—institutional bottlenecks, unlawful intake requirements, and broken referral loops.

Built to Document Contact — Not Adjudicate Cases
Continuity Contact Records document objective contact timestamps, actor identities, and handling statuses. They make no claims regarding evidence authenticity, substantive merit, or agency liability. All records remain survivor-held property under strict client-side encryption.
