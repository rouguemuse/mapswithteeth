# MAPS WITH TEETH

> **Barrier-First Resource Intelligence & Continuity Infrastructure**

Maps With Teeth is an investigative intelligence initiative and software infrastructure that helps survivors and advocates bypass conventional shelter dead-ends through lateral statutory rights, industry emergency benevolence funds, deposit waivers, and confidential relocation remedies—while preserving intake context across disconnected systems.

---

## 🎯 Current Product Maturity

- **Stage 1 (Live Demo Catalog)**: 47 Audited Public Records with 100% primary source and regulatory citations.
- **Stage 2 (Dynamic Fact Matcher)**: Deterministic qualification engine evaluating work history, legal status, and family levers without central PII storage.
- **Stage 3 (Continuity Infrastructure)**: Portable research dockets and encrypted survivor documentation packaging (In Active Design).

---

## 🧭 Repository Orientation

The repository is strictly structured into clear domain and architectural layers:

- **\`src/app/\`**: Public Next.js web application routes.
- **\`src/components/\`**: Interface components organized by domain (\`ui/\`, \`layout/\`, \`resources/\`, \`intake/\`, \`admin/\`).
- **\`src/domain/\`**: Pure business logic, qualification rules, matching engines, and resource publication rules.
- **\`src/data/\`**: Canonical structured resource records (\`resources/states/tx/\`, \`resources/national/\`), evidence provenance, and taxonomy.
- **\`src/content/\`**: Public editorial content, practitioner field notes, and statutory explainers.
- **\`research/\`**: Non-public investigative files, candidate leads, and interview notes.
- **\`prototypes/\`**: Experimental product prototypes (evidence organizer, routing graphs).
- **\`docs/\`**: Initiative vision, architecture standards, roadmaps, and decision records.
- **\`archive/\`**: Historical records, superseded plans, and deprecated research snapshots.

📖 **For full directory mappings, see [REPO_MAP.md](file:///docs/architecture/REPO_MAP.md).**
